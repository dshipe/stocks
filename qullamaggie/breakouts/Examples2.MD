# Qullamaggie Breakout Examples — Tickers & Dates

Kristjan Kullamägi (Qullamaggie) teaches three main setups: **Continuation Base Breakouts**, **High Tight Flags (HTF)**, and **Episodic Pivots (EP)**. His blog and streams teach the method rather than providing static ticker/date lists, but the following specific examples are well-documented across his content and community resources.

## Pattern Criteria

- **Continuation / High Tight Flag (HTF):** Stock surges 30–100%+ in 1–3 months → consolidates tightly (2–8 weeks) → breaks above consolidation high on elevated volume.
- **Episodic Pivot (EP):** Stock gaps up 10%+ on major news/earnings on massive volume → entry on break of the opening range high.

# Qullamaggie Breakout Setup — Historical Chart Examples

> These examples illustrate the classic Qullamaggie breakout setup across a variety of stocks and time periods.
> Use these as visual templates when scanning for future breakout candidates.
> For each example, the pattern structure follows: explosive prior move → tight base → volume contraction → breakout.

---

## How to Use These Examples

Each example documents:
1. **The prior move** — what happened before the base formed
2. **The base** — consolidation type, depth, duration
3. **Volume behavior** — how volume dried up
4. **Breakout** — the trigger, entry price, and result
5. **Outcome** — where the stock went post-breakout

Study these patterns until you can recognize them instantly on a chart.

## Example 1: Tesla (TSLA) — July 2020

**Type:** Flat Base / Cup-with-Handle Breakout  
**Timeframe:** Daily chart, July 2020

**Prior Move:**
- TSLA surged from ~$350 (February 2020 post-split equivalent) to ~$900 in early 2020
- After a COVID crash to ~$350, TSLA rebounded explosively, gaining 150%+ by June 2020
- Clear catalyst: S&P 500 inclusion speculation + electric vehicle sector boom

**The Base:**
- Post-surge, TSLA formed a cup-with-handle pattern from late April through June 2020
- Base depth: ~35% (cup was deeper but the handle was tight at ~10%)
- Handle duration: approximately 2 weeks
- Price held above the 50-day moving average throughout

**Volume Behavior:**
- Volume dried up significantly during the handle portion
- Multiple days of below-average volume in the 10 days before breakout
- No large distribution (high-volume selling) days in the handle

**Breakout:**
- Date: approximately July 13, 2020
- Breakout above ~$900 (pre-split) on 3× average daily volume
- Large green candle closing near the high of the day

**Outcome:**
- TSLA ran from ~$900 to ~$2,400 within 8 weeks (split-adjusted: $180 to $480)
- Multiple continuation setups formed as the stock climbed
- Classic example of letting a winner run with a trailing 10-day MA stop

**Chart Reference:** TradingView TSLA daily — focus on June–August 2020
**TradingView URL:** `https://www.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=TSLA` (navigate to July 2020, daily)

## Example 2: Nvidia (NVDA) — August 2020

**Type:** Flat Base / Earnings Catalyst Breakout  
**Timeframe:** Daily chart, August 2020

**Prior Move:**
- NVDA rallied from ~$180 (March 2020 COVID lows) to ~$480 by July 2020 — a 165% move
- Driven by data center demand, cloud computing growth, and AI chip sales
- Strong earnings beat in May 2020 fueled the initial surge

**The Base:**
- After hitting $480, NVDA formed a 5-week flat base from late June to early August 2020
- Base range: $390–$480 (approximately 18% depth — slightly deeper but justified by the size of the prior move)
- Volume contracted nicely throughout the base

**Volume Behavior:**
- Average volume during base was ~40% below the 50-day average
- Clear drying up of volume in the final week before breakout
- No distribution days (no down days on above-average volume)

**Breakout:**
- Date: August 19, 2020 (earnings + stock split announcement catalyst)
- NVDA broke above $480 on massive volume — 5× average daily volume
- Opened above the base and ran all day

**Outcome:**
- NVDA jumped from $480 to $589 in a single day (+20%)
- Over the following 6 months, NVDA rallied to $615+ (additional 25%+ from breakout)
- This is also an "episodic pivot" example due to the gap-up on catalyst

**Chart Reference:** TradingView NVDA daily — focus on June–September 2020

## Example 3: Abercrombie & Fitch (ANF) — Late 2023

**Type:** High-Tight Flag (HTF)  
**Timeframe:** Daily chart, November–December 2023

**Prior Move:**
- ANF surged 100%+ in under 8 weeks, driven by a massive earnings beat
- Catalyst: Q3 2023 earnings crushed estimates; management raised guidance significantly
- Went from ~$30 to ~$75 in weeks

**The Base:**
- After the surge, ANF formed a high-tight flag over 3 weeks
- Flag depth: approximately 12–15% from the high
- Very orderly: shallow drift lower on declining volume
- Held above the 10-day moving average throughout

**Volume Behavior:**
- Volume declined with each passing week of the flag
- Final 5 days before breakout: volume at or below 40% of average
- Classic VCP within the flag: 3 contractions of decreasing magnitude

**Breakout:**
- ANF broke above the flag high on 2.5× average volume
- Strong green candle, closed in the top 10% of the day's range

**Outcome:**
- +18% gain in the next 2 weeks
- Stop would have been the flag low (approximately 12% below entry)
- Risk/reward: approximately 1.5% risk, 18% gain = 12:1 R/R

## Example 4: Shopify (SHOP) — October 2020

**Type:** VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) Breakout  
**Timeframe:** Daily chart, September–October 2020

**Prior Move:**
- SHOP rallied from $300 (March 2020) to $1,100 by August 2020 (e-commerce COVID boom)
- Multiple episodic pivots on earnings throughout 2020
- Clear sector leadership in cloud/e-commerce

**The Base:**
- VCP structure: 3 contractions of decreasing size
  - First contraction: 22% depth
  - Second contraction: 14% depth
  - Third contraction: 6% depth (the tight pivot)
- Total base duration: ~7 weeks
- Each contraction also showed declining volume

**Volume Behavior:**
- Volume contracted noticeably with each consolidation leg
- Virtually no volume in the final contraction (VCP pivot)
- Breakout day: 3× average volume

**Breakout:**
- Price broke above the final VCP pivot on high volume
- Confirmed by end of day — strong close

**Outcome:**
- SHOP ran from ~$1,050 to ~$1,450 over the following 6 weeks (+38%)
- Trailing stop with 10-day MA would have captured most of the move

## Example 6: Palantir (PLTR) — November 2024

**Type:** Episodic Pivot (EP) + Continuation Breakout  
**Timeframe:** Daily chart

**Prior Move:**
- PLTR was range-bound for months before a massive earnings gap
- Q3 2024 earnings: massive beat on both revenue and EPS, raised guidance significantly

**The Episodic Pivot:**
- PLTR gapped up 30%+ on earnings day on 5× average daily volume
- This alone is an "episodic pivot" trade — enter on break of the opening range high
- Day's range was tight: stock opened and held near the high all day

**Continuation Base:**
- 3-week flat base formed right below the EP high
- Depth: 8%
- Volume dried up to below 50% of average

**Breakout:**
- PLTR broke above the EP consolidation high on 2× volume
- Triggered another leg up of 40%+ over the following weeks

**Outcome:**
- EP entry to continuation breakout = multi-leg, +80% combined move
- This is the ideal "staircase" progression Qullamaggie describes

## Example 8: The Generic Breakout Pattern (Template)

This template represents the ideal Qullamaggie setup structure for any stock:

```
QULLAMAGGIE IDEAL BREAKOUT — PATTERN TEMPLATE

Price Action:
                                                   /---  (Post-breakout run)
                                                  /
                                          -------/  ← BREAKOUT (high volume)
                                         /           ← Pivot price
               -------------------------              ← Tight base (low volume)
              /
             /   ← Prior explosive move (30-100%+, high volume)
            /
           /
----------/  ← Low point / catalyst day

Timeline: [Weeks/Months of base] → Breakout day → Multi-week run

Volume Profile:
||||||||||||||||         Low volume in base          |||||||||||||||||
                                                    ^ HIGH on breakout
     ^ High during prior move      ^ LOW in base

Key Measurements:
- Prior move: ≥ 30% gain
- Base depth: ≤ 10-15% from high to low
- Base duration: 5-40 days
- Volume in base: ≤ 60% of average
- Breakout volume: ≥ 150% of average
- Stop: below base low
- Target 1: Entry + 2× (Entry – Stop)
- Target 2: Trail with 10-day MA
```

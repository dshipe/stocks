## Background

Kristjan Kullamägi — known online as **Qullamaggie** — is an Estonian-born, self-taught momentum and swing trader who built a career in the U.S. stock market without any formal finance education.

He began trading in **2011** at the age of 23 with no prior market knowledge. Like many beginners, he started by following stock alerts, chasing momentum without a process, and quickly learned the hard way what not to do. In his first two years he blew up his trading account **three or four times**, funding continued attempts by working night shifts and, at times, taking out small loans.

## The Turning Point

Rather than quit, Kristjan turned his failures into a curriculum. He studied every resource he could find — including pirated DVDs from trading educators like Timothy Sykes — and reverse-engineered what actually worked. By **2013** he had finally achieved consistent profitability and never looked back.

As his skills matured he shifted from short-term day trading to **swing trading**, zeroing in on momentum-driven technical setups that could yield outsized returns over days to weeks. This evolution proved transformative: by **2017** he had achieved financial independence and reportedly built his trading profits into **tens of millions of dollars**.

## Philosophy

Kristjan's approach is deliberately simple and rules-based:

- Trade **price action and momentum**, not macroeconomic narratives.
- Master **a few high-probability setups** rather than dabbling in many.
- Cut losers **quickly and decisively**; let winners run as far as they will go.
- Risk management is non-negotiable — **never let one trade ruin your account**.

## Community & Content

Having reached financial independence, Kristjan began sharing his methods freely on **YouTube**, **Twitter/X**, and **Twitch** around 2017–2019. He has been explicit that he earns his living by trading, not by selling courses, and that his educational content is offered to genuinely help aspiring traders rather than exploit them.

He emphasises mentorship and community, frequently hosting live trading sessions, sharing annotated charts, and answering questions from followers.

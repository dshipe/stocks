## Background: The Supply/Demand Setup

The post was written during the COVID-19 market crash of Spring 2020. U.S. shale oil production had grown enormously in the prior decade, and much of that shale production yielded **associated natural gas** as a byproduct. This created a structural oversupply of dry gas, driving prices to unprofitable levels for most pure-play natural gas producers.

When the COVID-19 pandemic caused global oil demand — and thus oil prices — to collapse, many shale oil wells became uneconomic and were **shut in**. The knock-on effect: a significant reduction in associated gas production was expected to follow. Meanwhile, natural gas **demand had not fallen proportionally**, suggesting a coming supply-demand rebalance and a potential sharp price recovery.

---

## The Trade Thesis

1. **Oversupplied market about to rebalance** — oil well shut-ins would reduce associated gas supply.
2. **Stocks priced for bankruptcy** — many gas producers had been beaten down to near-zero, pricing in prolonged sub-$2 natural gas.
3. **Potential for extreme upside** — deeply discounted "turnaround" commodity stocks can produce multi-hundred-percent gains in a rebalancing cycle.
4. **Technical confirmation** — several beaten-down gas names had already begun bouncing, with some doubling in the preceding month.

## Execution Notes

- **Do not blindly chase** — wait for proper technical setups (breakouts, pullbacks, consolidations).
- Higher-quality names ($CNX, $EQT, $COG) offer **better risk/reward** because they are less likely to go bankrupt even if the thesis takes longer to play out.
- The **cyclical nature of commodities** means timing matters: enter when the technical setup confirms the fundamental thesis, not before.
- Use **tight stops** given the high volatility of commodity stocks.

---

## Key Lesson

> *"Deeply discounted turnaround stocks, priced for bankruptcy, can sometimes outperform even the best growth stocks over short bursts — but only if you wait for the technical confirmation and size the position appropriately for the risk."*

## Outcome Context

Natural gas prices did recover significantly through 2021–2022, and several of the stocks mentioned produced substantial gains for traders who timed the entry well.

## YouTube — Official Channel

**Channel:** [youtube.com/@Qullamaggie](https://www.youtube.com/@Qullamaggie)

Kristjan uploads edited educational content, setup breakdowns, and market commentary. Key content categories:

### Swing Trading School Series
A core educational playlist covering:
- How to find stocks using momentum scanners
- Setting up a proper pre-market routine
- Breakout setup mechanics with real chart examples
- Episodic Pivot identification and entry techniques
- Position sizing, stop placement, and scaling out
- Market context: when to be aggressive vs. when to stay in cash

### Setup Breakdowns
Deep dives into specific historic trades — wins and losses — explaining:
- Why the setup qualified (or why it looked like it would but failed)
- Entry, add-on, and exit execution
- What was done right vs. what could have been done better

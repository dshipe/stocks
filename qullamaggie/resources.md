## Recommended Books

These books have been referenced by Kullamägi as foundational to his trading education:

| Book | Author | Why It Matters |
|------|--------|----------------|
| *How to Make Money in Stocks* | William J. O'Neil | The foundational text on CAN SLIM methodology; explains buying breakouts from proper bases on volume |
| *One Up on Wall Street* | Peter Lynch | Framework for understanding company types and stages (fast growers, cyclicals, turnarounds, etc.) |
| *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* | Edwin Lefèvre (Jesse Livermore) | Classic narrative on trader psychology, position sizing, and letting winners run |
| *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* | Mark Minervini | Modern application of O'Neil principles; momentum trading and SEPA methodology |
| *The Battle for Investment Survival* | Gerald M. Loeb | Early classic on risk management and the importance of cutting losses |

## Typical Scan Parameters (Kullamägi-Style)

### Breakout Scan
- **Price:** > $5
- **Volume:** > 500k shares average daily volume
- **Performance:** Top 1–2% by 1-month, 3-month, and 6-month price change
- **Pattern:** Near 52-week highs; trading above 10-day and 20-day SMA
- **Volume today:** At least 1.5× average daily volume on breakout day

### Episodic Pivot Scan
- **Pre-market gap:** > 10% with at least 50k shares in pre-market
- **Catalyst:** Company-specific news (earnings, FDA, M&A, major contract)
- **Volume:** Trades average daily volume within first 15–20 minutes of regular session
- **Price:** Breaks above opening range high

### Momentum Continuation (Pullback) Scan
- **Recent move:** Stock up 30–100%+ in last 1–3 months
- **Pullback:** Price has pulled back to the 10-day or 20-day SMA
- **Volume:** Volume contracting on the pullback (dry-up signal)
- **Pattern:** Higher lows; tightening price range
